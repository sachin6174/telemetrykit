# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, appearance, nationality, or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and empathetic.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking impact seriously.
- Accept correction and make repair when harm occurs.
- Protect private information, credentials, and unpublished vulnerability details.
- Make room for contributors with different communication styles and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private or security-sensitive information are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is also prohibited.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, events, and private project communication.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through an owner-approved channel. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports as confidentially and promptly as practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and take action proportionate to context. Actions may include a clarification, warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent ban.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be removed from the project role.
